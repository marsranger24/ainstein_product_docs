_All specifications above are measured in an environment with 35 °C temperature, standard atmospheric pressure, and humidity without electromagnetic interference (EMI).  
The Operational Temperature Range indicates that radar works properly in this range. If the operating temperature goes beyond this range, the radar might not be accurate and can suffer mechanical damage._

<br />

- **Operational Temperature Range is based on the hardware’s subcomponent specifications. Actual operational testing is still pending.**

The US-D1 Pro will support accurate data within its 0.5 to 120 m altitude range. However, if the radar is mounted and operated below or above this range, its operation may vary.

If the US-D1 Pro is mounted below 0.5m, RF saturation may occur. This can lead to unexpected, unreliable readings.

During installation, testing, and observation, if unexpected behavior is observed, revise the distance between the US-D1 Pro and the ground. If the distance is below the range of operation of the US-D1 Pro, the behavior is caused by the explanation above. Following all safety measurements, users can continue testing and operating the US-D1 Pro in its designed range. If the unexpected behavior continues within the range, contact Ainstein’s Technical Support team.

Similarly, when the US-D1 Pro is operating above its designed range of operation, its readings will report as 0 m. If the unexpected behavior continues within the range, contact Ainstein’s Technical Support team.\*\*
