## 3.1 Obstruction to the LR-D1 Field of View

Objects or aircraft structures that are located within the LR-D1 Pro’s conical field of  
view which obstruct the radar’s view of the ground may cause multipath reflections or  
other degrative phenomena to occur. The LR-D1 Pro should be mounted a safe distance  
away from the landing gear and any other components of the aircraft below it.

## 3.2 Excessive Pitch/Roll

Pitch and roll angles that exceed 21.5° and 15° respectively may also cause the  
performance of the LR-D1 Pro to worsen. This sensitivity increases with altitude.

## 3.3 Terrain

Terrain with poor reflectivity may cause the performance of the LR-D1 Pro to worsen.  
Flying at the limit of the LR-D1 Pro’s range over dry, loose soil, such as tilled farmland,  
or sand is not recommended.

## 3.4 Power Source

Any power source used to operate the LR-D1 Pro that does not provide the minimum  
power and voltage can worsen the performance of the device or cause its operation to  
stop altogether.

## 3.5 Orientation of LR-D1 Pro

The LR-D1 Pro must be mounted upon the aircraft in such a manner as to be fully  
horizontal, its radome directly facing the ground. Any angle of inclination may degrade  
performance. The LR-D1 Pro will output an alert code “0800” when the angle of  
inclination of the radar is tilted beyond 20° during flight. If this alert is output while the  
aircraft is stationary on the ground, please adjust the radar orientation

## 3.6 Minimum and Maximum Operating Altitude

Operating the LR-D1 Pro at altitudes below 0.3 meters and above 500 meters will result  
in a degradation of performance and potentially erroneous measurements. Also, any  
reading at an altitude of greater than 655.35m would be considered as an error or  
overflowed reading (see Appendix 1).
