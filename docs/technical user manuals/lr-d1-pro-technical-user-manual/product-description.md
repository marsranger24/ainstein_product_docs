## 1. Product Description

**1.1 LR-D1 Pro**  
The LR-D1 Pro Radar Altimeter uses the principles of radio detection and  
ranging to determine the altitude of an aircraft. The LR-D1 features both a 24 GHz and  
60 GHz radar system, providing precise altitude measurements in takeoff and landing  
as well as in-flight up to 500 m in altitude. A microwave signal is transmitted out of the  
sensor, reflected off the target, and received by the sensor. Distance to the terrain and  
vertical velocity are derived by the difference in time from when the signal is sent from  
the sensor to when the signal is received by the sensor and by the Doppler effect.

1.3 Product Features  
The LR-D1 Pro contains several unique features that provide advantages to the  
user. The first of which is the dual-band antenna design. The LR-D1 Pro utilizes both a  
24 GHz and 60 GHz frequency system simultaneously. This allows for drastically finer  
precision below 5 m in altitude from the 60 GHz system while maintaining a range of  
up to 500 m from the 24 GHz system. A carefully designed algorithm switches between  
the two frequency systems to give the user the most precise above-ground-level (AGL)  
measurement possible. The redundancy of data at low altitudes makes for extra safety  
at critical landing or sea-skimming maneuvers of flight, which occur at 5 m altitude and  
below.  
The LR-D1 Pro also features solid-state electronics and cutting-edge patch  
antennas that allow the radar to have a smaller form factor and mass compared to  
traditional altimeters.  
The LR-D1 outputs instantaneous measurements at a high update rate of 15 ms  
(66 Hz), enabling the user to operate at airspeeds of 200 m/s and beyond.  
The LR-D1 Pro features a rugged mechanical design that maintains the radar  
from being affected by adverse weather, allowing for use on aircraft that operate in rain,  
snow, sea spray, over water, fog, or dust. Due to the FMCW radar employed, the  
altimeter can output accurate measurements in these conditions, whereas laser  
altimeters, sonar, and cameras cannot, making the LR-D1 Pro an ideal choice for aircraft  
that operate over bodies of water or in extreme conditions.  
The radome design of the LR-D1 Pro is specially tested to be radiative to radar  
signal transmission of both 24 GHz and 60 GHz frequencies, so customer customization  
of the enclosure is not recommended.

The LR-D1 Pro can connect and transmit data using CAN, RS-232, RS-422,  
RS-485, and TTL protocols, making integration into the user’s flight controller as simple  
and straightforward as possible
