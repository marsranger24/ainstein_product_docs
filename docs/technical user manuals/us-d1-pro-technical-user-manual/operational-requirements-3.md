## 3. Operational Requirements

The US-D1 Pro will perform optimally if the operational requirements below are fully  
satisfied. Failure to meet the operational requirements may cause a decrease in  
performance, accuracy, or reliability of the US-D1 Pro altimeter and is not advised.  
3.1 Obstruction to the US-D1 Pro Field of View  
Objects or aircraft structures that are located within the US-D1 Pro’s conical field of  
view which obstruct the radar’s view of the ground may cause multipath reflections or  
other degradative phenomena to occur. The US-D1 Pro should be mounted a safe  
distance away from the landing gear and any other components of the aircraft below it.
