## 1.1. US-D1 Pro Product Description

The US-D1 Pro is the second generation of **short-mid range Radar Altimeter**. It uses the principles of radio detection and ranging to determine the altitude of the aircraft. A microwave signal is transmitted out of the sensor, reflects off the target, and is received by the sensor. Distance from the sensor to the terrain (altitude) is derived from the difference in time from when the signal is sent from the sensor to when the signal is received by the sensor.

**1.2. Compliances**

**1.2.1 Radio Equipment, EMC/EMI, RF**

This equipment is designed to comply with the limits for a Class B device (commercial or industrial use), pursuant to Part 15 of the FCC rules or RED of 2014/53/EU. These limits are designed to provide reasonable protection against harmful interference when the equipment is operated in a commercial environment. This equipment generates, uses, and can radiate radio frequency energy and, if not installed and used in accordance with the instruction manual, may cause harmful interference to radio communication. Operation of this equipment in a residential area is likely to cause harmful interference, in which case the user will be required to correct the interference at his/her own expense. 

However, we have not performed the official test and have not obtained the grant of FCC ID from the authorities, nor do we have the CE Declaration of conformity ready yet.

## 1.2.2 Radio Technical Commission for Aeronautics (RTCA)

This equipment is designed to comply with **RTCA DO-160, 178, and 254.**

1. Environmental (DO-160) - This equipment is designed to comply with the RTCA DO-160G. However, we have not performed the official test and have not obtained the certification yet.
2. Software (DO-178) - This equipment is designed to comply with the RTCA DO-178C DAL C. However, we have not performed the official test and have not obtained the certification yet.
3. Electronic Hardware (DO-254) - This equipment is designed to comply with the RTCA DO-254 DAL C. However, we have not performed the official test and have not obtained the certification yet.
