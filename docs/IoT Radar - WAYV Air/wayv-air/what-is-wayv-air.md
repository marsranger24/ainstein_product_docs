---
title: What is WAYV Air?
excerpt: >-
  Wayv Air is a short-range radar used for occupancy detection or to count a
  small number of people indoors. It operates at a 60GHz frequency band, and its
  detection range is up to 6 m. This small size and lightweight makes it
  feasible to be installed/mounted on a ceiling or wall.
