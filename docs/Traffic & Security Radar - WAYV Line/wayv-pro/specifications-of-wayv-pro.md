Performance Notes:

  * Ethernet port: used for data
  
* Interface: 12V power supply, power consumption <40W, due to current impact, it is recommended that the power module is not less than 8A, it is recommended to use a 2.5mm cable, and the cable length does not exceed 20 meters, long traces and small diameters Will cause a large voltage drop, thereby causing    potential    power    supply risks
  
  * RS422 safety monitoring interface: used to monitor the safe operation status of the whole machine
  
  * output (optional): The radar can output 3 groups of independently controlled switches, with two states of on/off, used to trigger external alarm devices
  
  * output (optional) The radar can output 1 channel 12V/3A power supply for powering external alarm equipment
  
  * IO control output (optional): the radar can output 2 12V switch signals to control the on and off of external equipment
