Radar is the most reliable and affordable, all-encompassing solution.

- Plug and Play: No calibration required.
- Accurate in all Weather and Environmental Conditions: Dust, fog, over water, daytime/nighttime.
- Compact Design: Ainstein's radar altimeters range from 685 grams down to 110 grams.
- ArduPilot & PX4 Support:
  - The US-D1 is compatible with both PX4 and ArduPilot via UART, CAN, and DroneCAN.
  - The US-D1 Pro is compatible with open-source flight control software via DroneCAN.
  - The LR-D1 is compatible with ArduPilot via UART.
- Affordability: The price of Ainstein's radars ranges from $599 to $6,900.
- Rugged Design: All of Ainstein's radar altimeters are IP67 certified while the US-D1 Pro is IP69K certified.
